<h1><a id="features"></a>Features</h1>
🪟 Full Glassmorphic Redesign — Frosted translucent panels, soft depth, and rounded surfaces across the whole UI <br>
📚 Rebuilt Page by Page — Dashboard, Modules, Inbox, Announcements, Discussions, and navigation all redesigned <br>
🏙️ Custom Wallpapers — Upload your own, and watch it glow through every panel <br>
🎨 Any Accent Color — One picker recolors links, buttons, highlights, and active states everywhere <br>
🌊 Subtle Animations — Smooth hovers, expands, and transitions that guide you instead of distracting you <br>
🌙 Dark Mode — Switch Canvas to a darker palette with one click. Easier on the eyes for late-night study sessions <br>
📋 Todo List — All your upcoming assignments, quizzes, and deadlines laid out. Check things off as you go and never miss a due date <br>
🏆 Grades & Class Rank — See your grades at a quick glance, right on the dashboard. Get deeper insight into where you rank in the class, right in the grades page <br>
